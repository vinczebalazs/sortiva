Are these facts true, and are they facts?

Ten made-up shops, five in English and five in Hungarian, were read the way a real install would read them. Each product's text went through one model call that lists only checkable statements. A statement was kept only if it quotes its source word for word and every number in it appears in that quote. Below, for each shop, five products as the merchant wrote them, beside what we kept. 246 facts in all.

What to look for

  • A fact that the product text does not support, or that changes a number or a unit.
  • Marketing that slipped through as a fact (“perfect for”, “the best”, how the buyer will feel).
  • A plain, checkable detail in the description that we missed.
  • The Hungarian shops read as natural Hungarian, and their facts stay in Hungarian.

These are fixture shops, not the dev store: no dev store exists yet. The same page can be produced from the dev store once the app is installed there.

Drafted profile · what they sell

Fernhill Coffee Supply sells home coffee brewing equipment and coffee beans. The range covers pour-over gear (ceramic and stainless steel drippers, size 02 paper filters, stovetop and electric gooseneck kettles, and a brew scale with timer), immersion brewers such as a French press and a cold brew jar, and manual and electric burr grinders. It also sells single-origin coffee beans from Fernhill Roastery in 250 g and 1 kg bags, whole bean or ground.

Who buys it

People who brew coffee at home, especially by pour-over or immersion, and who want to grind, weigh and control water temperature themselves. Some products, such as the single-dose flat-burr grinder and the temperature-hold kettle, suit buyers who care about precise brewing.
