How it works

Find, act, learn.

01

Find

Your catalogue is read with read-only Shopify access and stripped to a fact sheet per product. That is joined to your Search Console history and live market data, and scanned weekly for the queries and pages where you have room to win.

02

Act

Every opportunity arrives with a chosen action and the evidence behind it. Articles are written and published — or exported — for you. Page recommendations are yours to apply in Shopify; we never touch your theme.

03

Learn

Each acted-on opportunity is measured against its own before-figures after 28 days, and the result feeds the next scan. Decay, cannibalisation and broken references come back as new opportunities rather than silent rot.

The quality bar

Three gates. Not posting is a valid outcome.

Gate 1 · before planning

Is the topic worth it?

Search volume in your language and country, difficulty against what your site actually ranks for, whether your catalogue holds enough facts to say something, and whether you already have a page targeting it. Costs nothing to fail.

Gate 2 · before drafting

Is there anything to say?

The article is written only from an evidence pack assembled first: fact sheets, live search results, competitor angles. A pack that's 90% boilerplate is killed before a word is drafted.

Gate 3 · before publishing

Is it better than what ranks?

Duplicate and stuffing lints, then a separate grader scoring information gain and factual grounding against the pages currently ranking. Both are hard floors. One revision attempt, then the draft is held back and you're told why.

Why one a day, and why "up to".

A daily ceiling is the most a store can absorb before the archive starts repeating itself. It's a ceiling, not a quota: a day with no topic that clears the bar produces nothing, and the calendar says so in place.

Thin pages tend to cost the domain that hosts them, so the plan is priced against the ceiling and the product is measured against the bar.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will anything be published without me seeing it first?

You'll always have had the chance to. The full three-month plan is visible from the day you confirm your profile, and any topic can be deleted, moved or pinned right up until it starts generating. If you'd prefer to approve each draft before it goes live, there's a toggle in settings for that.

Can I edit an article inside Sortiva?

Not in the app. Review is kept to a single decision — approve or discard — so there's no editor here. Published articles can be edited in Shopify like any other post, and exported ones arrive as Markdown and HTML you're free to change.

What happens on a day with nothing good to write about?

Nothing publishes that day. The calendar marks it as held back, names the check that stopped it, and where there's something you could do about it — usually a few missing product details — it tells you what. The daily limit is a ceiling, so an empty day is a normal outcome.

Does it work if I'm not on Shopify?

Shopify only for now. Best-seller figures and publishing both come through the Shopify Admin API. You're welcome to paste your URL — we'll tell you right away whether we can work with your store, and we're happy to talk if you'd like a custom setup.

Do you need permission to write to my store?

Not unless you want auto-publishing. Setup asks for read-only access, and export is the default: you download the article and publish it yourself. Write access is a separate permission, requested only when you switch auto-publishing on.

Do I have to connect Search Console?

It's optional. Without it, topics are scored on search demand alone. With it, planning can see which of your articles earned traffic and weight the next quarter towards what worked — and you get per-article reporting.

How is this different from bulk AI content?

The unit here is a topic rather than a product, so a single guide can cover a whole family of similar items. Articles are written from facts taken off your own product pages, and every draft is graded against the pages currently ranking before it's cleared. Output is capped at one a day.

What happens to my articles if I cancel?

Articles you've already published stay on your store, untouched. Generation and publishing stop at the end of the period you've paid for, and you keep read access to your articles, calendar and reporting.

Nothing publishes that you couldn't have seen coming.
