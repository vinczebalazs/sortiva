Cleaning a dripper takes a minute and keeps every cup tasting clean.

Every day

  • Rinse the dripper with hot water straight after brewing.
  • Tip the used filter and grounds into compost.
  • Let it dry upside down.

Once a week

  1. Soak it in warm water with a little washing-up liquid.
  2. Scrub the ribs with a soft brush.
  3. Rinse twice, then dry.
